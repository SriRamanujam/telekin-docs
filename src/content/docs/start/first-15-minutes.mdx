By the end of this page, Claude will have written down what it learned about you and your work, and a brand-new chat will already know it.

## Set up your account

You'll need a Telekin account. Your invite is an email with a link to set it up: open it, choose a password or a passkey, and you're signed in. [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/) walks you through it. Keep the address the link starts with, for example `https://telekin.example.com`: it's your Telekin address.

<Waitlist />

This page uses Claude on the web (claude.ai), because it needs no terminal. If you use another app, pick it from [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/) and come back for the later steps.

## Connect Claude

<Steps>

1. Go to **Customize, then Connectors** (claude.ai/customize/connectors).
2. Click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
3. Name it `Telekin`, paste your Telekin address with `/mcp` on the end (for example `https://telekin.example.com/mcp`) as the **Server URL**, and click **Continue**. If you're asked how to authenticate, choose **OAuth** (a way of signing in through a page in your browser; there's no password to paste). Leave any client ID or client secret fields empty.
4. Claude opens Telekin's page in your browser. If you're not signed in, sign in with your Telekin account first.
5. The page, **Allow access?**, names the app that's connecting. Tick your memory (Telekin's screens call it a catalog), choose **write** so Claude can save what it learns, pick the same memory as the **Default catalog**, and choose **Approve**. Your browser returns to Claude, now connected. (**Deny** connects nothing.)
6. In a chat, click **+**, then **Connectors**, and make sure `Telekin` is on.

</Steps>

You should now see `Telekin` in the connectors list, switched on for that chat.

## Add one line to your preferences

Claude only uses Telekin when it thinks of it, so give it a nudge. Add this to your custom instructions (in Claude, your profile's personal preferences, under Settings). Menu names move around, so search settings for "preferences" if you can't find them:

> I have a knowledge catalog (Telekin) connected that holds long-term
> context about me. Search it before answering whenever I mention a project, system,
> person or problem, and use what you find openly. When we establish
> something worth remembering, record it there.

## Let it interview you

Start a new chat and paste this:

> I've connected a knowledge catalog that's meant to hold long-term context
> about me, so every AI I use knows my world. Interview me to fill it: ask
> me one question at a time about my work, the projects I'm on, the tools
> and systems I use, the people I work with, and how I like to work. After
> every few answers, record what you've learned in the catalog as concepts
> (open a changeset, create or update concepts, then publish). Start now.

Answer as much or as little as you like. Claude asks one question at a time and, every few answers, saves what it learned. Claude may ask your permission before each save; allow it. At the end, ask "What did you record?" to see what went in.

A changeset is just a batch of edits Claude saves in one go.

## See it remember

<Steps>

1. Start a **new chat**, so nothing from the interview is in front of Claude.
2. Ask: **"What do you know about me?"**

</Steps>

You should see Claude search Telekin and then summarize what it found, in its own words. Now ask a work question that names one of your projects. Notice whether it uses what it already knows instead of asking you to explain.
