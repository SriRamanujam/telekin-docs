You already have written-down context: a README, your notes, a project brief, your own bio. In this tutorial you hand one document to your AI and watch the useful parts land in Telekin.

You'll need an AI app already connected to Telekin. If you haven't done that yet, start with [Your first 15 minutes](/start/first-15-minutes/).

<Aside type="caution" title="Keep it non-confidential">
  Don't paste anything your employer considers confidential. Personal projects, your own tools and setup, how you like to work, and public information about your job are all fine.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Pick one document** you have on hand, such as a README, a project brief or your own bio. Short is fine.

2. **Start a new chat and paste the document.** Under it, say:

   > Record the durable facts from this in my catalog.

   Your AI works out which facts will still matter later, and saves them to Telekin. It may ask your permission to save; allow it.

3. **Ask what it saved.** Type "What did you record?" You should get a list of what went in. If something is missing or wrong, say so and ask it to fix it.

4. **Open a new chat and ask about it.** Choose something the document covered and ask about it without pasting anything. You should see your AI search Telekin and answer from what it recorded.

</Steps>

That's the whole loop: paste, record, ask. Repeat with another document whenever you like.
