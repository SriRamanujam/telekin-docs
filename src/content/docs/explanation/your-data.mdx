Your Telekin memory can hold personal things, so here is exactly where it stands today.

## Storage

Your memory is stored on Telekin's server. It is encrypted in transit: what travels between your AI app and the server can't be read along the way.

## Who can see it

You, with your key, and the operator of the Telekin server. The operator can technically read catalogs. Nobody else has a key to yours. Treat your key like a password.

## Training

Telekin doesn't use your data to train AI models.

## Your AI client

Telekin is only half the picture. When your AI app reads from Telekin, what you see goes into that chat, and what the app does with it afterwards is governed by that app's own policy. That means Anthropic's for Claude, OpenAI's for ChatGPT and Codex, Google's for Gemini, and the maker's policy for Cursor, VS Code and the rest. Check the privacy settings of the apps you connect.

## Export and deletion

Both are on request today. <a href={CONTACT_URL}>Ask us</a> and you get a full copy of your memory, or your memory is deleted. A self-serve export is planned.

## Format

Your memory is plain Markdown files, in an open format. You own it and can take it anywhere: any text editor can read it, and nothing about it is locked to Telekin.

Not sure what to keep out? See [Keep private material out of Telekin](/how-to/guides/keep-private-material-out/). Other questions are in the [FAQ](/faq/).
