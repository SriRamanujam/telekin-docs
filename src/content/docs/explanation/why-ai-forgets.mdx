You connected Telekin, you asked a question it should have known, and your AI answered as if it had never heard of you. The memory is there. The AI didn't look. This page explains why, and what helps.

## Why AIs don't always check

Connecting Telekin gives your AI a set of tools. An AI only uses a tool when something prompts it to. Nothing in a fresh chat says "you have a memory, look in it first", so unless your question or your settings point that way, the AI answers from what it already knows and never opens the tool.

Long chats make it worse: instructions given early can fade as the conversation grows.

## What helps

- **Custom instructions (the extra line).** Add one short instruction to your AI app's settings, telling it to search Telekin whenever you mention a project, system or person, and to record what is worth keeping. See [Get your AI to use Telekin without being asked](/how-to/guides/use-without-asking/).
- **Name things in your question.** "Why is the billing service slow?" gives your AI a name to search for. "Why is it slow?" doesn't.
- **Ask it to check.** "Check Telekin for what we know about `<project>` before answering" works every time.
- **The Claude Code hook.** Claude Code can run a small startup script that reminds it, at the start of every session, which catalog is yours. Together with instructions in its global settings, it makes Claude Code check Telekin on its own. See [Connect Claude Code, enhanced](/how-to/connect/claude-code-enhanced/).

## Planned

Improvements on the Telekin server side, to make AIs more likely to check without help, are planned. There is no date for them yet. Until then, the steps above are the reliable ones.
