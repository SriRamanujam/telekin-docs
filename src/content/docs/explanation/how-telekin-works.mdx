Telekin is a memory that your AI apps share. This page explains the ideas behind it, so you know what to expect.

## The idea: one memory, many AIs

Most AI apps remember only what you told that app. Telekin keeps your memory in one place that sits outside any single app. Think of it as a hub with spokes: the memory is the hub, and each AI app you connect (Claude, Claude Code, Cursor and others) is a spoke that reads from it and writes back to it. Tell one AI something and the others can know it too.

The apps reach Telekin through MCP, the Model Context Protocol, an open standard that lets an AI app use outside tools. See [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/) for the apps that work today.

## Concepts

Your memory is made of **concepts**: one page for each project, system, person or decision. A concept is plain text that your AI and you can both read. Concepts link to each other, so an AI that finds your bike-shop website can follow a link to the calendar it uses and the decision behind it, and get a connected picture instead of loose snippets. For an example, see [What a concept looks like](/reference/what-a-concept-looks-like/).

## Drafts

Every change starts as a draft, called a changeset. Your AI collects its edits in the draft and nothing is visible, to you or to your other AIs, until the AI **publishes** it. Until then the draft can be inspected or thrown away. This is why an AI can write freely without leaving half-finished or wrong notes behind.

## Provenance

Each concept records who wrote it and when, and can list the sources a fact came from: a document, a conversation, a check against the real thing. When two AIs share one memory, that matters: you can see who wrote what, and how far to trust it. Concepts can also carry open questions, such as "is this still true?", for someone to answer later.

## History

Every publish is kept, with a short message saying what changed. You can ask your AI to show the history of any concept and bring an earlier version back. Reverting is itself a draft, so you can look at it before it goes live. Nothing is lost by a bad edit. See [Review, undo and export](/how-to/guides/review-undo-export/).

## Upkeep

A memory that only grows becomes a junk drawer. Telekin has checks your AI can run for concepts that have grown too long, that haven't been looked at in a long time, or that nothing links to, and for broken links. It can also move, rename or split concepts and keep the links working. You can ask for this with a prompt from the [Prompt library](/reference/prompt-library/).
