Each prompt below has a copy button. Paste it into any AI app that's connected to Telekin. Replace anything in `<angle brackets>` with your own words.

## The interview

Use this the first time, to fill an empty memory.

```text
I've connected a knowledge catalog that's meant to hold long-term context
about me, so every AI I use knows my world. Interview me to fill it: ask
me one question at a time about my work, the projects I'm on, the tools
and systems I use, the people I work with, and how I like to work. After
every few answers, record what you've learned in the catalog as concepts
(open a changeset, create or update concepts, then publish). Start now.
```

## Fill it from a document

Paste a document first (a README, your notes, a project brief), then say this.

```text
Record the durable facts from this in my catalog.
```

## What do you know about me?

```text
What do you know about me?
```

## What did you record?

Ask this after a chat where your AI saved something.

```text
What did you record?
```

## Check before answering

```text
Check Telekin for what we know about <project> before answering.
```

## Remember this

```text
Remember this: <what you want kept>
```

## What's out of date?

```text
What's out of date in Telekin about <project>?
```

## Show the history and undo

```text
Show me the history of <concept> and revert the last change.
```

## Tidy up

```text
Tidy up Telekin: find concepts that are too long or have broken links.
```

For what each of these does behind the scenes, see [What your AI can do](/reference/tools/).
