Short answers, each pointing to the page with the full story.

## Is my data used to train AI?

No. Telekin doesn't use your data to train AI models. The AI app you connect has its own policy, though, so check its privacy settings. See [Your data](/explanation/your-data/).

## Who can see my data?

You, with your key, and the operator of the Telekin server, who can technically read catalogs. Nobody else has a key to yours. Everything is encrypted in transit. See [Your data](/explanation/your-data/).

## What will it cost?

It's free during the private test. Pricing for after the test isn't final; we'll tell you before anything changes.

## Which AIs work with it?

Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI and Cline work today. Claude on the web and desktop and ChatGPT connect with a one-time sign-in code (OAuth); developer tools use a key. See [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/).

## Can I self-host it?

Yes. Telekin runs on okf-mcp, which is open source (AGPL-3.0), and every feature is in the open-source version. Look for the okf-mcp project by name.

## Can I delete everything?

Yes, on request: see [Your data](/explanation/your-data/). You can <a href={CONTACT_URL}>ask us</a> and your memory is deleted, or you get a full copy first. A self-serve export is planned. See also [Review, undo and export](/how-to/guides/review-undo-export/).

## Does it work on my phone?

Connectors you add to Claude on the web or desktop should show up in Claude's mobile apps, so set it up there first. Other apps on phones aren't confirmed yet. See [Connect Claude](/how-to/connect/claude/).

## Does it work offline?

No. Telekin is a hosted server, so your AI needs an internet connection to reach your memory.

## Can my team share a catalog?

Not yet. Teams are planned. For now, a catalog belongs to one person and one key. See [How Telekin compares](/explanation/how-telekin-compares/) for what's planned.

## How is it different from my AI's built-in memory?

Built-in memory belongs to one app, and you can't really inspect or version it. Telekin is one memory that several AIs share, stored as plain Markdown you can read, undo and export. See [How Telekin compares](/explanation/how-telekin-compares/).

## How is it different from Obsidian or other note tools?

A vault lives on one machine and mixes your notes with your AI's. Telekin is hosted, and keeps the AI's memory separate, with drafts, history and undo. See [How Telekin compares](/explanation/how-telekin-compares/).

## Where do I get a key or a sign-in code?

From your invite. Telekin is in a private test, so if you don't have one yet, join the waitlist. You get a key for developer tools and sign-in codes for Claude on the web and ChatGPT. A sign-in code works once, for one connection, so ask for another if you connect more apps. Once you have them, [Connect your AI](/how-to/connect/) shows where each goes.
