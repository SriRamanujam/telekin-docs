ChatGPT can't connect to Telekin yet.

<Waitlist />

## Why not

ChatGPT's custom connectors only accept OAuth sign-in. Telekin uses keys today, and OAuth support is planned.

## Meanwhile

Use [Claude](/how-to/connect/claude/) or [Cursor](/how-to/connect/cursor/). Your memory lives in Telekin, not in any one app, so whatever you set up there will be waiting when more apps are supported.
