## My setup link doesn't work

The page says **This link has expired. Ask for a new one.** A setup link works once, and only for 24 hours, and a newer one replaces it. If you already set up your account with it, just sign in instead. Otherwise, ask the person who invited you to send a new setup email. See [Set up your account](/start/set-up-your-account/).

## I forgot my password

On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot your password?**, type your email and choose **Send the link**. The email's link works once, for 1 hour. Choosing a new password signs you out everywhere. If you set up a passkey, you can also choose **Sign in with a passkey** instead.

## It keeps saying "Email or password is incorrect"

Check the email address and password for typos first. After 10 wrong passwords in a row, your account is locked for 15 minutes, and the page shows the same message while it's locked, on purpose. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password.

## The connector won't connect

Check that the URL is your Telekin address with `/mcp` at the end. In a developer tool that uses a key, also check that the header value starts with `Bearer ` (with a space). In Claude on the web or ChatGPT, you sign in in your browser instead of sending a header.

## The sign-in stopped partway

Signing in and approving must finish within 10 minutes of Telekin's page opening. If the page says the request has expired or is not valid, start again from the app: add the connector or choose connect again, and the page opens afresh.

## A memory isn't in the list

When you approve an app or make a key, Telekin offers only the memories your account reaches: your teams' memories, at the level each team allows, plus any memory shared with you. If a memory is missing, or only **read** is offered, your account doesn't reach it at that level. The **Account** page lists what it reaches under **Your ceiling**. Ask the person who invited you for access. If the approval page says you have no catalogs to share yet, your account reaches none so far.

## Making a key is refused

The message names the limit: more access than your account has, more keys than you're allowed, or an expiry past the longest allowed. Change that choice, or revoke a key you no longer use, and try again. See [Make a key](/how-to/guides/make-a-key/).

## A connection that worked has stopped

In your Telekin console, check **My keys** (for a developer tool) or **My grants** (for Claude on the web or ChatGPT).

- A key may have **expired** or been **revoked**. Tick **Show revoked and expired keys** to see it. Make a new key and put it in the tool in place of the old one.
- A connection that's gone from **My grants** was revoked. Remove the connector in the app, add it again and approve it.
- If your account's access was lowered, the key or connection reaches less than before. See [A memory isn't in the list](#a-memory-isnt-in-the-list).

## The AI says it has no tools

In Claude, make sure the Telekin connector is switched on in this chat: click **+**, then **Connectors**. In other apps, check the app's MCP settings for Telekin and restart the app.

## The AI has the tools but never uses them

It needs a nudge. See [Get your AI to use Telekin without being asked](/how-to/guides/use-without-asking/).

## Free plan: can't add a second connector

Claude's free plan allows one custom connector. Remove the one you don't need, or use a different app for the second connection. To change a connector's settings later, remove it and add it again.

## A header seems to be ignored

If you ever have access to more than one catalog, the header that picks them is `X-OKF-Catalogs` (plural), for example `X-OKF-Catalogs: t01`. A misspelled header is silently ignored, with no error. Most people don't need this header at all, because the key already points at your catalog.
