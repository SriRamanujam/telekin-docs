## What not to store

- **Anything your employer considers confidential.** Keep work secrets out, even if your AI offers to save them.
- **Secrets**, such as passwords, API keys and tokens. Telekin refuses obvious secrets, but don't rely on that. Don't paste them into a chat that has Telekin connected.

Personal projects, your own tools and setup, how you like to work, and public information about your job are all fine.

## Remove something

Ask your AI to delete it, or to revert the change that added it. For example: **"Delete what you saved about `<concept>`."**

Telekin keeps a history of changes, so the old text can still be in the history. If it must be gone completely, <a href={CONTACT_URL}>ask us</a> to purge it from the history too.

## Who can see it

Only you (with your key) and the operator of the Telekin server. The operator can technically read catalogs. Everything is encrypted in transit. Treat your key like a password. For the full picture, see [Your data](/explanation/your-data/).
