You don't need a special screen for any of this. You ask your AI, and it uses Telekin's tools.

## Review what was saved

Ask:

- **"What did you record in Telekin today?"**
- **"Show me the history of `<concept>`."** Replace `<concept>` with the name of the item you care about, such as a project.

Telekin keeps a history of every change, including which AI made it.

## Undo a change

Ask: **"Revert your last change to `<concept>`."**

Your AI looks the change up in the history and reverts it. The old version comes back, and the revert is itself recorded.

## Export everything

Ask us for a full export. You'll get your whole memory as plain Markdown files, which you can read in any text editor. Self-serve export is planned.
